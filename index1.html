⚠️Chemotherapy Dosing Safety Rule2 items▾

Board trap · Chemo is mg/m², never a universal mg/kgTempting: converting a chemotherapy dose to mg/kg like other pediatric drugs.
Right: all pediatric antineoplastic treatment is protocol- and BSA-based (mg/m²) — a protocol regimen, never a universal mg/kg. Do not invent an mg/kg equivalent when none is given; calculate BSA when the protocol supplies mg/m².
Body surface area (for mg/m² protocols)
BSA (m²) = √[(height cm × weight kg) / 3600]
Calculate it whenever the protocol supplies the dose in mg/m².
120 cm, 25 kg: 120 × 25 = 3000; 3000 / 3600 = 0.833; √0.833 = 0.91 m²
